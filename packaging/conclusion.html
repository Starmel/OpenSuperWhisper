OpenSuperWhisper is installed

The app is in /Applications, and everything it needs to run is inside it: the whisper speech engine and the llama.cpp transform engine are linked into the same binary, and neither one needs Homebrew, a background server or a listening port. The package carries no model weights, so it stays small and an update ships no weights again.

The models download on first use

Dictation needs a speech model, and translation or tone need a language model, one per output direction: about 1.6 GB for the speech model, about 1 GB for English output and about 5 GB for Polish output. Turn on Translate into … or Apply tone in Settings → Transcription, press Download model next to the direction you use, and the app fetches the weights into its own folder, verifies the pinned checksum, and keeps them there. Until then, dictation in that direction is pasted unchanged — one direction's model never stands in for the other's.

Uninstalling

/Applications/Uninstall OpenSuperWhisper.command, or Settings → Advanced → Uninstall OpenSuperWhisper…, removes the app, this uninstall command, the models the app downloaded and its caches — and stops there. Your recordings, your transcriptions and your settings are kept, so a later install finds them again.

To remove those too, run it with the one flag that does that:

  • /Applications/Uninstall OpenSuperWhisper.command --remove-user-data

It leaves ~/models, /opt/homebrew and every other application's data untouched, and running it twice is harmless.
